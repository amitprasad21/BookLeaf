# Author Experience

**App:** `author-frontend/` (Next.js, port 3000). **Users:** BookLeaf authors (seeded accounts, role `author`).

**Job to be done:** "Show me how my books and royalties are doing, let me raise a problem in under a minute, and tell me, without my having to check back, when BookLeaf responds."
