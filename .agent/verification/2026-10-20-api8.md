# 2026-10-20: API-8 live updates (SSE) and the portals' switch from polling

Branch `feature/api-sse` (from `main` at `a60195e`). Decision D-033.

## What was built

- **`packages/shared/src/events.ts`:**
  - Event types and change names.
  - `formatSseEvent` (identifiers only), an incremental SSE parser (chunk boundaries, CRLF, comments, multi-line data), and `toTicketEvent` (rejects unknown types and bad JSON; drops unknown change names).
- **Backend:**
  - `events/hub.ts`: in-process hub. Admins get every event; authors only `ticket.updated` for their own tickets (the publisher passes the ticket's `author_id`). At most 5 streams per user; one failing stream never blocks others; `closeAll()` on shutdown.
  - `events/ticket-events.ts`: a PATCH's audit actions become `ticket.updated` (status) and/or `ticket.internal_updated` (category, priority, assignee).
  - `modules/events/router.ts`: `GET /api/v1/events` for any signed-in user.
    - Headers: `text/event-stream`, `no-cache, no-transform`, `X-Accel-Buffering: no`.
    - Opens with `: connected`, sends `: ping` every 25 s, and ends after 10 minutes so the client reconnects with a current token. 429 past the per-user limit.
  - Publishing, after the write succeeded:
    - Author create → `ticket.created`.
    - Admin PATCH → `ticket.updated` / `ticket.internal_updated`.
    - Reply → `ticket.updated` (`messages` and `status`; the author's id is looked up, and if that fails only admins are told and a warning is logged).
    - Note → `ticket.internal_updated` (`notes`); AI re-run → `ticket.internal_updated` (`ai`).
    - Each finished triage job → `ticket.triaged` (from the queue wrapper in `server.ts`).
  - `openapi.yaml` `/events`: recipients, change names, heartbeat, lifetime, 429.
- **Portals** (`lib/event-stream.ts`, same file in both; `providers/LiveUpdatesProvider.tsx` per portal):
  - One stream per signed-in tab, read with `fetch` (token in the header). It refreshes the session once on 401.
  - Backoff 1, 2, 5, then 15 s. A long-lived stream that ends reconnects at once and stays "Live"; one that ends within 5 s counts as a failure.
  - Every open re-fetches what's shown (catch-up).
  - Author: `ticket.updated` re-fetches My Tickets and that ticket.
  - Console: every event refreshes the queue's comparison copy (so "Show N updates" appears), the summary, and the ticket concerned. Drafts are never refetched by events.
  - Pages stop polling while live, and the indicator reads "Live"; they go back to polling every 15 s ("Refreshing every 15 s") while the stream is down.
