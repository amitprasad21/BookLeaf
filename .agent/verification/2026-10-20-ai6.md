## Findings and follow-ups (not done here)

1. **Free-tier rate limits under a burst.**
   - About 12 triage calls in a minute exhausted Gemini's free per-minute quota, and the Groq fallback ran out soon after.
   - In production this means a burst of new tickets can leave some in `failed` until the sweeper retries (by design), or in manual review after three attempts.
   - Options: pace the triage queue under the per-minute quota, or use the paid tier for the deployed demo (A-18).
2. **Triage prompt:** add facts-reading rules like the draft prompt's, and say that a question about where a book is sold is not an outage. This needs a prompt version bump and a live re-run.
3. **Draft prompt, missing royalty:** state the 45-day window and the next payout date from the facts, ask the author to confirm bank details, and quote the pending amount. Same process: bump, re-run.
4. **Wrong overdue quarter (found in the 2026-10-20 browser checks):** drafts for a ticket on BK011 called the July–September 2026 payout overdue, though it is due 14 November 2026. The overdue flag concerns April–June. Put the overdue quarter and its deadline in the facts, and tell the prompt never to call the current quarter overdue.
5. **Production timelines:** the KB has no stage durations, so drafts can't give a grounded timeline. Adding typical durations to the KB (from BookLeaf) would fix it.
