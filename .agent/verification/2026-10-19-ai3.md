## 1. What was built (decision D-029)

- **`packages/shared/src/triage.ts`:** `TriageResultSchema` (strict: six categories, four priorities, confidence 0–1, at most 5 of 16 signals, rationale 10–300 characters), `TRIAGE_SIGNALS`, `LOW_CONFIDENCE_THRESHOLD`. Also `businessDayStart()` in the payout calendar.
- **`backend/src/ai/facts/triage-facts.ts`:** today; the ticket's book or, for General tickets, the author's totals; the payout calendar and overdue heuristic. No name, email, phone or city.
- **`backend/src/ai/prompts/triage.ts`:**
  - A static system prompt: categories with tie-breaks, rubric, signals, the injection rule.
  - Facts and ticket as JSON inside `<facts>`/`<ticket>`, with `<` and `>` escaped.
  - A repair note, a keyword mock responder, and prompt version `triage-2026-10-19.1`.
- **`backend/src/ai/validation/floors.ts`:** legal → critical, ISBN error → high, overdue payment → high. Floors only raise priority, and the rule is recorded.
- **`backend/src/ai/triage.ts`:** the job.
  - Load → facts → prompt → route, with one repair attempt.
  - Then floors → save, or the failure state, under a version lock (reload and re-apply on a conflict, no second AI call).
  - Audit `ticket.triaged` with labels only.
- **`backend/src/ai/queue.ts`:** in-process queue (concurrency 2, dedupe, close/idle); sweeper at startup and every 5 minutes, skipping while AI can't serve.
- **`backend/src/ai/control.ts`:** enqueue, availability and manual retry for the routes; `disabledTriage` for tests and AI off.
- **System writer:** `loadTicketForTriage`, `saveTriageResult` (admin values never replaced), `markTriageFailure`, `resetTriage`, `listTicketsForSweep`.
- **Router:** `availability()` (AI off, budget, every circuit open).
- **Providers:** get a schema without length/range/count keywords; zod enforces those after parsing.
- **Routes:** `POST /author/tickets` queues triage after the save. New `POST /admin/tickets/{id}/triage`:
  - 202 `{ data: { aiStatus: "pending" } }`; a pending ticket gets a no-op 202.
  - Limits: 3 per ticket per India business day (429 until midnight) and 5 per minute per admin.
  - 503 `AI_UNAVAILABLE` when AI can't serve.
- **`server.ts`:** AI router → triage job → queue → sweeper; graceful shutdown drains for 8 s.
- **Config:** `AI_TRIAGE_TIMEOUT_MS` default raised from 15 s to 30 s (live calls took 8–13 s).
